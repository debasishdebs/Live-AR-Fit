LiveFit is a voice control for the Live AR Fit workout app on your Rokid glasses. Ask Hi Rokid to start, pause, resume or end a workout, play or pause music, skip to the next or previous song, or switch the glasses display to your stats, route map, music controls or playlist. Ending a workout is always confirmed on the glasses. Requires the Live AR Fit app on the glasses and its phone app with a connected watch.

## Phone

**Short description** (80 characters max):

```
Live workout stats on your AR glasses, with heart rate and GPS from your watch.
```

**Full description** (4000 characters max):

```
Live AR Fit puts your workout in front of your eyes. Your Wear OS watch tracks heart rate, steps, distance and your GPS route; your phone runs the workout and shows it live on your AR glasses, so you never have to look down at your wrist.

Works with Rokid AR glasses. You need an Android phone, a Wear OS watch and, for the heads-up display, Rokid AR glasses set up with the Hi Rokid app. The glasses app is free and installed separately (see the project page).

WHAT YOU GET
• Live heads-up display: time, heart rate, distance, speed, steps and calories on your glasses.
• Walk, run and ride workouts tracked on your Wear OS watch with Health Services.
• Live route map on the glasses and the watch, with your phone's GPS as a fallback.
• Start, pause and stop from the phone, the watch, a glasses gesture or your voice.
• Offline voice commands: recognised on your phone, on-device only. Audio is never uploaded.
• YouTube Music control: see what's playing and play, skip or like from your glasses and watch.
• Pages you choose and touchpad gestures you configure, per page.
• Workout history on your phone, with heart rate and route.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics.
• Live AR Fit has no server of its own. Your workouts stay on your phone and watch.
• Map tiles come from MapTiler, which sees your IP address and the map area shown.
• Delete everything with Clear history, or by uninstalling.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Live AR Fit is open source under the Apache License 2.0. Source, glasses app and install guide: github.com/debasishdebs/Live-AR-Fit

Live AR Fit is a fitness app, not a medical device. Heart-rate readings come from your watch's sensor and are for training feedback only.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Map tiles © MapTiler.
```

The Wear OS companion of Live AR Fit. Your watch tracks the workout; the Live AR Fit phone app shows it live on your AR glasses.

Requires the Live AR Fit phone app. Works with Rokid AR glasses.

ON YOUR WRIST
• Walk, run and ride workouts with heart rate, steps, distance, speed and calories from Health Services.
• GPS route with "Use GPS outdoors" on, and a live map page.
• Glance, Workout, Stats, Playlist, Map and Music pages; scroll with the bezel or swipe.
• Start, pause and stop on the watch, or follow a workout started from the phone, glasses or voice; the workout stays one tap away as an Ongoing Activity.
• Control YouTube Music playing on your phone.
• Records offline and syncs to your phone when it's back in range.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
